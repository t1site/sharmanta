Linguo çarmanta

Official Grammar

Chapter 1 — General Principles

Article 1 — Language Name

The name of this language shall be Linguo çarmanta.

Article 2 — Nature of the Language

Linguo çarmanta is an international auxiliary language based on Latin and the Romance languages.

Article 3 — Basic Word Order

The basic word order shall be SVO (Subject–Verb–Object).

Article 4 — Parts of Speech

This language uses nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, articles, pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions, and interrogative words.

Article 5 — Grammatical Gender

Grammatical gender does not exist.

Article 6 — Plural Forms

The plural form of a noun is formed by adding -es to the end of its singular form.

Article 7 — Word Order of Modifiers

  1. Adjectives follow the nouns they modify.
  2. Adverbs follow the verbs they modify.

Chapter 2 — Part-of-Speech Endings

Article 8 — Nouns

The basic ending of nouns shall be -o.

Article 9 — Adjectives

The basic ending of adjectives shall be -a.

Article 10 — Verbs

Verbs take endings according to tense and grammatical function.

Article 11 — Adverbs

The basic ending of adverbs shall be -u.

Article 12 — Other Parts of Speech

Parts of speech other than those specified in the preceding articles shall end in a consonant.

Chapter 3 — Articles

Article 13 — Definite Article

The definite article shall be las.

The definite article is used for both singular and plural forms.

Article 14 — Indefinite Articles

The indefinite articles shall be as follows:

The plural form of a personal pronoun is formed by adding -es to the end of its singular form.

Possession is expressed by adding -is to the end of a personal pronoun.

Article 18 — Subject and Object Pronouns

The same forms of personal pronouns shall be used for both the subject and the object.

Chapter 5 — Verbs

Article 19 — Tense Endings

Verb tense endings shall be as follows:

TenseEnding
Present-e
Future-en
Past-et

Article 20 — Infinitive

The infinitive form of a verb is formed by adding -ir to its stem.

Article 21 — Comparative and Superlative Forms

The comparative form of a verb is formed by adding -er, and the superlative form by adding -est.

Article 22 — Priority of Verb Endings

When multiple endings are added to a verb, the following order shall be observed:

  1. Tense ending
  2. Infinitive ending
  3. Comparative or superlative ending

Article 23 — Dictionary Form

Verbs shall be listed in their present-tense forms in dictionaries.

Article 24 — Negation

Negation is expressed by placing non immediately before the verb.

Article 25 — Imperative Sentences

Imperative sentences shall be formed according to the following rules:

  1. The subject shall be omitted.
  2. ze shall be placed at the beginning of the sentence.
  3. The verb shall take the future-tense form.

Article 26 — The Verb “To Be”

The verb meaning “to be” shall be estae.

estae shall be treated as a regular verb and not as an irregular verb.

Article 27 — Comparative Expressions

When expressing a comparison using the comparative form of a verb, the following structure shall be used:

(verb)+er kit (comparison target)

Example:

Jo corer kit tuo.

I run faster than you.

Chapter 6 — Questions

Article 28 — Question Marker

The question marker Çet shall be used in interrogative sentences.

Çet shall be placed at the beginning of the question.

Article 30 — Position of Interrogative Words

Interrogative words shall be placed at the very beginning of a sentence.

Article 31 — Word Order in Questions

The basic SVO word order shall also be maintained in interrogative sentences.

The conditional clause shall precede the main clause.

A comma shall be placed between the conditional clause and the main clause.

Article 39 — Stress

Stress shall always fall on the second-to-last vowel of a word, without exception.

Chapter 10 — Orthography

Article 40 — Writing System

This language shall use the Latin alphabet.

Article 41 — Capitalization

Capital letters shall be used in the following cases:

  1. The first letter of a sentence.
  2. The first letter of a proper noun.

Article 42 — Lowercase Letters

All letters not covered by the preceding article shall generally be written in lowercase.
